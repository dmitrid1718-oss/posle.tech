What happens
afterward?

Does an encounter with intelligence leave us more curious, more capable of independent thought, more present with the people around us?

Or does it quietly make us more dependent on the machine?

We measure engagement, satisfaction, and performance. We rarely measure what a person carries back into ordinary life.

POSLE is a place to explore that overlooked space. No conclusions to defend. No promise that technology makes us better. Just a question worth investigating.
